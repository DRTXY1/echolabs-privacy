Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

EchoLabs is a Twitch Extension that lets viewers on a channel send text-to-speech messages that are read aloud and shown on the broadcaster's stream. This policy describes what data is processed, why, and who it is shared with.

1. What data we process

From viewers (Panel component):

  • The message you type (sent to ElevenLabs to generate the spoken audio)
  • The voice you select
  • Optionally your Twitch display name — but only if you explicitly grant Twitch's "Share your identity" permission prompt. If you decline, an anonymous, randomly generated label is shown instead, which cannot be traced back to your Twitch account.
  • Your Bits or Channel Points transaction is processed entirely by Twitch itself — we never receive your payment or account details.
  • Your language preference is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so the extension shows up in your language on your next visit. This information never leaves your browser.

From the broadcaster (Config panel):

  • The broadcaster's ElevenLabs API key is stored securely on our server and is never exposed to viewers.
  • All settings the broadcaster configures (voices, prices, word filter, overlay design, cooldowns, access restrictions) are stored server-side to run the extension for that channel.
  • Broadcasters can optionally connect their own Discord webhook to receive a notification for every TTS message sent. If enabled, the message content and (if shared) the viewer's name are sent to the Discord server the broadcaster chose.

Automatically:

  • To prevent abuse (e.g. enforcing cooldowns, rate limiting), a requesting device's IP address may be processed briefly server-side. It is not stored long-term or used for any other purpose.

2. How we use this data

  • To convert your text message into spoken audio and play it on the stream
  • To display your message and (optional) display name in the broadcaster's overlay
  • To enforce the broadcaster's configured settings (word filter, cooldowns, access permissions, maximum message length)
  • To operate and maintain the extension

We do not sell data to third parties and do not use your messages for advertising or to train AI models.

3. Third-party sharing

ElevenLabs (speech synthesis) — receives the message text and the broadcaster's API key to generate the audio. ElevenLabs Privacy Policy ↗

Twitch — the platform the extension runs on. Bits and Channel Points transactions are handled entirely by Twitch. Twitch Privacy Notice ↗

Railway (hosting) — our server and hosting provider, where broadcaster configuration data is stored. Railway Privacy Policy ↗

4. Data retention

  • Message text and the audio generated from it are processed in real time and not stored permanently — they are discarded after playback in the overlay.
  • Broadcaster configuration data remains stored until the broadcaster changes it or requests deletion (see Contact below).

5. Children's privacy

EchoLabs is not directed at children under the age of 13. Twitch's own age requirements for using Extensions apply.
